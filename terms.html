Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2, 2026

1. Overview

House Bread ("the App") is a home affordability calculator designed to help users estimate what they may be able to afford when purchasing a home. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

House Bread does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Specifically, the App does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact information
  • Financial data, income figures, or any numbers you enter into the App
  • Location data or GPS information
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or tracking data
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or behavioral data
  • Cookies or any local storage beyond what is required to run the App

All calculations performed within the App occur entirely on your device. No data is sent to any server, cloud service, or third party at any time.

3. One-Time Purchase

House Bread is offered as a one-time paid purchase ($5.99) through the Apple App Store and Google Play Store. Payment processing is handled entirely by Apple or Google, respectively. House Bread does not receive, store, or process your payment information.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or social media services. There are no third-party SDKs embedded in the App that collect user data.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. Because the App collects no data from any user, it inherently does not collect data from children.
