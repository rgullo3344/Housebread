Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 3, 2026

1. Overview

House Bread ("the App") is a home affordability calculator that helps users estimate what they may be able to afford when purchasing a home. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App does and does not handle. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the App and manage subscriptions.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

House Bread does not ask you to create an account and does not collect, store, or share your personal information. Specifically, the App does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or other contact information
  • The financial figures you enter into the App (income, expenses, home price, and so on)
  • Location data or GPS information
  • Advertising IDs or advertising and tracking data
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data

The numbers you enter are used only to perform calculations inside the App on your device. They are not sent to us or to any third party.

3. Subscription and Payments

House Bread is free to download. Full access requires an annual subscription ($9.99 USD per year, which may vary by country), and new subscribers may receive a 7-day free trial. Subscriptions are purchased through the Google Play Store or the Apple App Store, which process all payments. We never receive or store your credit card or other payment details.

To confirm whether your subscription is active, the App and a third-party subscription management service we use may receive limited purchase information, such as a purchase token, your subscription status and dates, and a randomly generated app user ID. This information is not linked to your name or email address by us. Google and Apple handle your payment information under their own privacy policies.

4. Website Hosting

The web version at housebread.xyz is hosted by Vercel. Like most hosting providers, Vercel may automatically log standard technical information about requests, such as IP address, browser type, and the time of the request, to deliver and secure the service. We do not use this information to identify you.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third parties, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play and the Apple App Store (app distribution and payments)
  • A third-party subscription management service (subscription status checks)
  • Vercel (hosting of the web version)

The App does not include advertising networks or analytics tools.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Choices

Because we do not collect personal information, there is no account or profile for us to delete. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through your Google Play or Apple App Store account settings. Uninstalling the App removes it from your device.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.
